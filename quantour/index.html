About

QuanTour Project

The QuanTour project was part of the International Year of Quantum Science & Technology 2025 (IYQ2025), led by UNESCO. Over the course of 12 months (Apr 2024 – Apr 2025), an operational semiconductor single-photon emitter travelled across 12 European countries and subsequently to laboratories across North America, Asia, Africa, and Australia.

#QuanTour · #QTorch · #SUPERquantum

What was delivered

  1. Communication strategy and coaching

    Built the campaign strategy from scratch with the organisers: audience definition, core narrative, channel and format selection. Coached Doris Reiter and Tobias Heindel on sustainable communication practice and on working with digital platforms they had not used before.

  2. Under the Microscope podcast series

    A 14-episode series — the two organisers plus all 12 QuanTour Heroes. Roughly 30 minutes each, covering the research, the career path behind it, the project each scientist is proudest of, and three wishes for improving the research experience. The organisers described the result as "a kind of lecture series on quantum science, accessible to everyone."

  3. Social media campaign

    Instagram-first storytelling following the source itself as the protagonist, from zero to 4,800+ followers over the tour. Video, shorts and stills produced at lab handovers and public events.

  4. Press and media relations

    Coordinated press releases, news features, radio and journal coverage across the 12 host institutions — 30+ publications in 9 languages.

  5. Live events and moderation

    Kick-off event at PTB Berlin (April 2024) and the World Quantum Day 2025 return event with the DPG at Urania Berlin, plus conference talks presenting the QuanTour model to the scicomm and quantum communities.

  6. Engagement reporting

    Five engagement reports issued across the project lifecycle, tracking reach, audience growth and coverage — used by the consortium for internal steering and funder reporting.
