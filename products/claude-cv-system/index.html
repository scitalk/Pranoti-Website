What this guide covers · Key highlights

A system, not a template

Generic CV templates and keyword-stuffing tools don't hold up to a hiring manager's scrutiny. This guide teaches a repeatable research-and-positioning process instead.

Approval gates
Claude never silently rewrites your CV
Four checkpoints — job analysis, strategy, rewrite, and format — each requiring your explicit approval before Claude proceeds. You control every strategic decision.
Built into every step
Peer profiling
Research 3–5 people already in the role
Job postings are often generic. Claude looks up people currently in similar roles at the employer to find the pattern in what actually gets hired for.
Most valuable research step
No fabrication
Real metrics only — qualitative signals otherwise
Claude is instructed to never invent numbers. If you don't have a metric, it uses scale, novelty, recognition, or scope instead. Strategic positioning, not misrepresentation.
Authenticity by design
