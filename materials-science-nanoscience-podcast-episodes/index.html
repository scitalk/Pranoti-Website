About Under the Microscope

Under the Microscope is the flagship podcast of The Science Talk, bringing the people and ideas shaping materials science and nanoscience to a wider audience.

Produced and hosted by Dr. Pranoti Kshirsagar, with a PhD materials and nanoscience and over a 15 years of experience in research and innovation, the podcast puts scientists—not just their discoveries—at the centre of the conversation.

Each episode features a leading scientist working in materials science or nanoscience. Through in-depth, accessible conversations, we explore their research, groundbreaking discoveries, the challenges behind their work, and the stories that shaped their scientific journey.

The goal is simple: to make exceptional science visible, understandable, and connected to the people behind it.

Under the Microscope is independently produced and self-funded by Dr. Pranoti Kshirsagar.
