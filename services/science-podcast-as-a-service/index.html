How it works

  1. 1

    Scoping call

    We agree the angle, the audience, and which package fits. You get a short brief so you know exactly what we will cover.

  2. 2

    Recording

    One remote session, recorded in broadcast-quality audio and video. No studio, no travel, no equipment needed on your side.

  3. 3

    Production

    Editing, sound design, colour, captions, episode artwork, show notes, and metadata. You approve before anything goes out.

  4. 4

    Release

    Audio goes live on Under the Microscope. Video goes live on The Science Talk YouTube channel. Both are free to access, instantly.

  5. 5

    Promotion

    Announcement posts, short-form video, and a personal shoutout from Pranoti — followed by repeat promotion rounds at three and six months on the larger packages.

Our SciComm Approach

There are two ways to do science communication. The innovation approach puts the product or the company first. The scientist approach puts the person doing the research first. Under the Microscope follows the scientist approach — on every episode, whoever is funding it, it is the scientist who is the guest and the research that is the subject. Topics span materials science and nanoscience through to life sciences and the natural sciences more broadly.

Who this is for

  • Research groups and PIs with a result that deserves an audience beyond the journal
  • EU-funded projects and consortia with a dissemination work package to deliver
  • Institutes and universities building a public science presence without hiring a production team
  • Deep-tech and instrumentation companies who want credibility with a scientific audience
