ERC Synergy Grant Recipients (2018–2025)

Every ERC Synergy Grant funded between 2018 and 2025, drawn from the European Commission's own grant data. Filter by call year and research domain, and see how many proposals were submitted against how many were funded each round.

All schemes Starting Grants Consolidator Grants Advanced Grants

The ERC Synergy Grant funds a group of two to four principal investigators, at any career stage, who jointly address an ambitious research problem that none of them could tackle alone. It is the ERC’s largest single award and its smallest scheme by number of grants — a few dozen a year.

This page lists the funded projects for every Synergy Grant call from 2018 through 2025 — the principal investigators, host institutions, countries, research domain, and a direct link to the project record on CORDIS. Use the filters to narrow by call year or by domain.

There was no Synergy call in 2021, during the transition from Horizon 2020 to Horizon Europe. Grant amounts, durations, and the application process are set out in the ERC Work Programme and on the ERC’s own site.
