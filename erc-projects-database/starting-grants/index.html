ERC Starting Grant Recipients (2016–2025)

Every ERC Starting Grant funded between 2016 and 2025, drawn from the European Commission's own grant data. Filter by call year and research domain, and see how many proposals were submitted against how many were funded each round.

All schemes Consolidator Grants Advanced Grants Synergy Grants

The ERC Starting Grant supports researchers who defended their PhD more than 0 and up to 10 years ago and are starting or have recently started their own independent research team. The eligibility window can be extended for parental leave, long-term illness, national service and similar documented circumstances. It is the ERC scheme that attracts the most applicants each year, and the one most early-career researchers in Europe measure themselves against.

This page lists the funded projects for every Starting Grant call from 2016 through 2025 — the researcher, host institution, country, research domain, and a direct link to the project record on CORDIS. Use the filters to narrow by call year or by domain (Physical Sciences & Engineering, Life Sciences, Social Sciences & Humanities).

The 2016–2020 calls ran under Horizon 2020; from 2021 onward, under Horizon Europe. Full eligibility rules, extensions, grant amounts and durations are set out in the ERC Work Programme and on the ERC’s own site.

How hard is it to get one?

The table below shows funded projects against submitted proposals for each call. Success rates have sat between roughly 10% and 15% for most of the last decade — the exact figure moves with the size of the applicant pool and the available budget.
