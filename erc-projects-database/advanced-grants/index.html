ERC Advanced Grant Recipients (2016–2024)

Every ERC Advanced Grant funded between 2016 and 2024, drawn from the European Commission's own grant data. Filter by call year and research domain, and see how many proposals were submitted against how many were funded each round.

All schemes Starting Grants Consolidator Grants Synergy Grants

The ERC Advanced Grant supports established research leaders. The ERC expects an active researcher with a track record of significant research achievements. There is no PhD-based eligibility window — the scheme is open to researchers at any career stage, and the test is the strength of the record and the ambition of the proposed work.

This page lists the funded projects for every Advanced Grant call from 2016 through 2024 — the researcher, host institution, country, research domain, and a direct link to the project record on CORDIS. Use the filters to narrow by call year or by domain (Physical Sciences & Engineering, Life Sciences, Social Sciences & Humanities).

The 2025 Advanced Grant results are not yet in this database: those grant agreements have not been signed, so the projects have not appeared in the European Commission’s dashboard. The ERC has published the 2025 Advanced Grant results list (PDF) in the meantime.

Grant amounts, durations, and evaluation rules are set out in the ERC Work Programme and on the ERC’s own site.
