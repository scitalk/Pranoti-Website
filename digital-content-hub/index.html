Under the Microscope

KISS of Light

Episode 210 · 2023

Antonija Grubisic-Cabo

University of Groningen

Antonija Grubisic-Cabo studies electronic band structure, the property that decides whether a material behaves as an insulator, a semiconductor or a metal. She looks at what makes materials behave the way they do electronically, and at ways to change those properties both in and out of equilibrium.

Antonija studies two-dimensional materials: crystals that are atomically thin and consist essentially only of surface, which makes them behave very differently from their 3D parent compounds. She uses photoemission spectroscopy to probe their electronic properties and explores how those properties shift when the substrate underneath or the surrounding environment changes, looking for new physics and future electronic devices. She arrived in the field mostly by accident, after a planned PhD in magnetism did not materialise.
