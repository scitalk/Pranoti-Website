## About the guest

Rajesh Ramanathan was a Senior Research Fellow at RMIT University when we recorded this episode. He is now a [Professor and Deputy Head (Research and Infrastructure) in the Department of Applied Chemistry and Environmental Science at RMIT University](https://www.rmit.edu.au/profiles/r/rajesh-ramanathan), and co-leads the NanoBiotechnology Research Laboratory. His research develops nanozyme technologies, rapid point-of-care diagnostics and antimicrobial materials.

Rajesh Ramanathan curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from September 28 to October 4 2020. [Read all of Rajesh's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g42).
