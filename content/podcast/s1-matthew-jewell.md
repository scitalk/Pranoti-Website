## About the guest

Matthew Jewell was an Associate Professor at the University of Wisconsin–Eau Claire when we recorded this episode. He is now [Chair of the Materials Science and Biomedical Engineering Department](https://uwec.edu/profiles/jewellmc) there. His group studies the mechanical properties of superconducting materials for large magnets: YBCO tapes and Bi-2212 and Nb3Sn round wires.

Matthew Jewell curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from December 9 to December 14 2019. [Read all of Matthew's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g11).
