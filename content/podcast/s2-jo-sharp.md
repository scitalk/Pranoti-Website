## About the guest

Jo Sharp is a Research Fellow in Advanced Characterisation at the University of Sheffield, UK, investigating the microscale internal structure of alloys, including 3D-printed parts for aircraft engines, usually with electron microscopy. Current work looks at what happens when additively manufactured metals are heated and pressed to close trapped gas pores. Jo studied Natural Sciences at Cambridge.

Jo Sharp curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 23 to November 29 2020. [Read all of Jo's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g47).
