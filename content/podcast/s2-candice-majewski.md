## About the guest

Candice Majewski is an Academic in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Sheffield, UK, researching additive manufacturing (3D printing). A favourite project introduced antibacterial properties into 3D-printed parts using a silver-based material, bringing together 3D printing and microbiology. A guest lecture during her Mechanical Engineering degree at Loughborough University set her direction.

Candice Majewski curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from June 7 to June 13 2020. [Read all of Candice's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g30).
