## About the guest

Craig Bunt is an Associate Professor at Lincoln University, New Zealand, researching controlled-release formulations for veterinary and human medicines and agricultural products, with a current focus on electrospun nanofibres and 3D printing. A highlight is taste-masking a wildlife control compound, which helped food security and endangered species recovery. He trained in pharmacy and worked in veterinary pharmaceutics research in industry.

Craig Bunt curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from April 26 to May 2 2020. [Read all of Craig's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g25).
