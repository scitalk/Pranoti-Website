## About the guest

El-Abed Haidar is a PhD student at The University of Sydney, Australia, using quantum mechanical first-principles calculations to identify carbon-based structures for molecular electronics, sensors, semiconductors and superconductors. Past projects ranged from nonlinear optics to a molecule that acts as a switch in a molecular device. The academic journey began in engineering in Lebanon.

El-Abed Haidar curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from March 29 to April 4 2021. [Read all of El-Abed's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g58).
