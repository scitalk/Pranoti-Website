## About the guest

Loreta A. Muscarella was a PhD candidate at AMOLF when we recorded this episode. She is now an [Assistant Professor in Physics and Astronomy at the Faculty of Science of Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam](https://research.vu.nl/en/persons/loreta-muscarella/).

Loreta A. Muscarella curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from May 24 to May 30 2021. [Read all of Loreta's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g63).
