## About the guest

Pablo Ares is an Assistant Professor in the Condensed Matter Physics Department at the Autonomous University of Madrid, Spain, advancing atomic force microscopy and studying 2D materials and van der Waals heterostructures. A highlight is the first isolation of antimonene, a single layer of antimony atoms. He studied physics at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid and worked as an application scientist at Nanotec Electrónica.

Pablo Ares curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from June 14 to June 20 2021. [Read all of Pablo's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g64).
