## About the guest

Thomas Swift is a Lecturer in Polymer Chemistry at the University of Bradford, UK, studying functional polymers, both biological and synthetic, for uses from removing substances from solutions to diagnostics, adhesives and property modifiers. A highlight is a bacterial sensor published in 2019. Thomas worked in R&D for a paint manufacturer before an industrially sponsored PhD in Sheffield.

Thomas Swift curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from March 22 to March 28 2020. [Read all of Thomas's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g22).
