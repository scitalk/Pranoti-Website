## About the guest

Sofia Masi is a [Senior Researcher at Universitat Jaume I](https://www.inam.uji.es/users/sofia-masi) in Castelló, Spain, where she leads the laboratory for synthesis and fabrication of optoelectronic devices at the Institute of Advanced Materials (INAM). Her current research focuses on stabilising photoactive perovskite crystal phases that are otherwise unstable.

Sofia Masi curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from July 5 to July 11 2021. [Read all of Sofia's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g67).
