Dr. Martin Rejhon is advancing fundamental graphene research at Charles University Prague—particularly epitaxial graphene on silicon carbide. His work on strain-engineered graphene opens scalable pathways toward future optoelectronics and advanced electronic devices.

## Key Takeaways

**🔬 Graphene Epitaxy** — Epitaxial graphene growth via thermal decomposition of silicon carbide enables scalable production techniques.

**⚙️ Straintronics Innovation** — Controlled strain (rather than twist angles) can tune graphene properties, opening pathways to novel electronic and optoelectronic applications.

**🔍 Precision Characterisation** — Raman spectroscopy and the Park Systems NX10 AFM are used to precisely map graphene structures and quality.

**🏭 From Lab to Fab** — Dr. Rejhon's vision: translating graphene research from fundamental science to large-scale industrial applications.

**🌐 NSFE 2026 Prague** — Hosting the NanoScientific Forum Europe 2026, with expert talks, lab tours, and networking in Prague.
