## About the guest

Sherif A. Tawfik Abbas was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at RMIT University when we recorded this episode. He is now a [Senior Research Fellow at Deakin University's Applied Artificial Intelligence Initiative (A2I2)](https://experts.deakin.edu.au/56124-sherif-abbas), which he joined in 2021 as an Alfred Deakin Postdoctoral Research Fellow. His work sits at the crossroads of materials science, physics, chemistry and AI.

Sherif A. Tawfik Abbas curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 29 to December 6 2020. [Read all of Sherif's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g48).
