## About the guest

Steph Gallant is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Montreal, Canada, using vibrational spectroscopy to study plasmonic materials, metals with nanoscale features, for sensors. A highlight is the cobalt ferrite sensor experiments, which uncovered unexpected crystal growth. Steph has done research since 2014, starting during a bachelor's at Memorial University in Newfoundland.

Steph Gallant curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from May 23 to May 29 2022. [Read all of Steph's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g92).
