## About the guest

Niklas Friedrich is a PhD Student at CIC nanoGUNE, Spain, creating one-atom-thick nanographenes, flat molecular structures of carbon hexagons, designed to be magnetic. His first first-author paper made the cover of Physical Review Letters. He studied physics in Aachen and Santiago before his PhD in Donostia.

Niklas Friedrich curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from October 18 to 24 2021. [Read all of Niklas's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g76).
