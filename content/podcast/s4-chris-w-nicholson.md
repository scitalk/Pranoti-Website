## About the guest

Chris W. Nicholson is a Group Leader at the Fritz Haber Institute of the Max Planck Society in Berlin, Germany, researching how electrons behave in solids, where many electrons crowded together give rise to new properties. The path ran from St Andrews to a PhD in Berlin, a postdoc in Switzerland, and back to Berlin.

Chris W. Nicholson curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from February 7 to 13 2022. [Read all of Chris's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g83).
