## About the guest

Brian R. Pauw is a Researcher at BAM, the Federal Institute for Materials Research and Testing, Germany, using X-ray scattering to work out the fine structure of materials and how it shapes their behaviour. Highlights include reimagining the data corrections for X-ray scattering and supporting a broad range of scientists on the lab's instrument. He studied chemical engineering at TU Eindhoven and did a PhD at DTU, Denmark.

Brian R. Pauw curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from March 14 to March 20 2022. [Read all of Brian's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g86).
