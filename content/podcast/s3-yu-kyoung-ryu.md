## About the guest

Yu Kyoung Ryu was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Polytechnic University of Madrid when we recorded this episode. She is now an [Assistant Professor in the Department of Applied Physics and Materials Engineering at the Polytechnic University of Madrid (UPM)](https://portalcientifico.upm.es/en/ipublic/researcher/332919), researching graphene for energy applications and thermal scanning probe lithography at the Institute of Optoelectronic Systems and Microtechnology.

Yu Kyoung Ryu curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from July 19 to July 25 2021. [Read all of Yu's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g69).
