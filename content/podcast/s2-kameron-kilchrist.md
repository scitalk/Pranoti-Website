## About the guest

Kameron Kilchrist is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Janssen Research & Development, USA, a biomedical engineer designing better drug delivery technologies using science at every scale, from proteins to whole organisms. A recent highlight is a new assay that measures endosomal disruption in live cells, a key step in how nanoparticle drug carriers deliver their payload. The path ran through a tissue engineering lab and computational image analysis.

Kameron Kilchrist curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from January 26 to February 1 2020. [Read all of Kameron's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g16).
