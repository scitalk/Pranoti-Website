## About the guest

Drew Evans was an Associate Research Professor at the University of South Australia when we recorded this episode. He is now Professor and [Dean of Research in the College of Engineering and IT at Adelaide University](https://adelaide.edu.au/people/drew.evans), having moved with UniSA to the new Adelaide University in 2025. His research group in the Future Industries Institute works on the performance and manufacturability of materials for industry, including commercialising conducting polymers for energy storage.

Drew Evans curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from September 30 to October 5 2019. [Read all of Drew's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g1).
