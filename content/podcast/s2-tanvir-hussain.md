## About the guest

Tanvir Hussain was an Associate Professor at the University of Nottingham when we recorded this episode. He is now an EPSRC Research Fellow and [Professor of Coatings and Surface Engineering in the Faculty of Engineering at the University of Nottingham](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/engineering/people/tanvir.hussain).

Tanvir Hussain curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from March 8 to March 14 2020. [Read all of Tanvir's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g21).
