## About the guest

Olga Shimoni is an Associate Professor and PI at the University of Technology Sydney, Australia, studying how nanotechnology can improve medical devices, especially for detecting disease early. The project closest to a commercial product is an easy, non-invasive screening test for celiac disease developed by her team.

Olga Shimoni curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from October 21 to October 26 2019. [Read all of Olga's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g4).
