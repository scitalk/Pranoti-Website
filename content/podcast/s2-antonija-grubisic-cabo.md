## About the guest

Antonija Grubisic-Cabo was a postdoc at KTH Royal Institute of Technology when we recorded this episode. She is now an [Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Science and Engineering at the University of Groningen](https://www.rug.nl/staff/a.grubisic-cabo/?lang=en).

Antonija Grubisic-Cabo curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from October 12 to October 18 2020. [Read all of Antonija's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g43).
