## About the guest

Vivian Tong is a Higher Research Scientist at the National Physical Laboratory (NPL), UK, using electron microscopy to study the microscale crystal structure of materials and developing electron diffraction techniques. Vivian's PhD in Materials Science used electron backscatter diffraction to study zirconium alloys used in nuclear reactor fuel tubes.

Vivian Tong curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from April 19 to April 25 2020. [Read all of Vivian's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g24).
