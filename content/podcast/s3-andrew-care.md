## About the guest

Andrew Care was a Group Leader and Chancellor's Research Fellow at the University of Technology Sydney when we recorded this episode. He is now an [Associate Professor and Academic Director of the Biologics Innovation Facility in the School of Life Sciences at UTS](https://profiles.uts.edu.au/Andrew.Care). His research combines synthetic biology and nanomedicine to re-engineer biologically derived nanoparticles for drug delivery and vaccines.

Andrew Care curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from May 17 to May 23 2021. [Read all of Andrew's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g62).
