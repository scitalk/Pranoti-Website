## About the guest

Eleonora D'Elia is a [Senior Teaching Fellow in the Department of Materials at Imperial College London](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/eleonora.delia10). She teaches undergraduate courses and develops the department's outreach, including summer schools, work experience programmes and school visits.

Eleonora D'Elia curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from August 17 to August 23 2020. [Read all of Eleonora's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g37).
