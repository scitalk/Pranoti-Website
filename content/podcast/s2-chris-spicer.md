Chris Spicer was a Lecturer in Chemistry at the University of York when we recorded this episode. He is now a [Reader in Chemistry at the University of York](https://www.york.ac.uk/chemistry/people/dr-christopher-spice/).

Chris Spicer curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 16 to November 22 2020. [Read all of Chris's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g46).
