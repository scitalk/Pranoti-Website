## About the guest

Upulie Divisekera is a Researcher at the University of Auckland, New Zealand, and Co-founder of Real Scientists. The doctoral work looked for new ways to deliver drugs to specific cells using tiny gold carriers. Earlier work in cancer immunology included an anti-CD73 immunotherapy that shrank tumours in mice and is now in clinical trials.

Upulie Divisekera curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from May 17 to May 23 2020. [Read all of Upulie's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g28).
