## About the guest

Kerstin Neuhaus is a Group Leader at Forschungszentrum Jülich, Institute of Energy and Climate Research IEK-12: Helmholtz Institute Münster, Germany, using a small conductive tip to measure and image charge transport at interfaces in energy materials. She studied Earth Sciences at the University of Münster, where her bachelor's thesis examined weathering of samples from the El Hammami meteorite.

Kerstin Neuhaus curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from April 24 to May 1 2022. [Read all of Kerstin's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g89).
