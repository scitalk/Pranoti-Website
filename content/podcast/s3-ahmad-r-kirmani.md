## About the guest

Ahmad R. Kirmani was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory when we recorded this episode. He is now a [professor in the School of Chemistry and Materials Science at Rochester Institute of Technology](https://www.rit.edu/science/news/kirmani-presents-perovskite-radiation-tolerance-pvspace-24), where his work includes the radiation tolerance of metal-halide perovskites.

Ahmad R. Kirmani curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from September 20 to 26 2021. [Read all of Ahmad's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g75).
