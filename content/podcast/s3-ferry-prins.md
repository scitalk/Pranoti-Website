## About the guest

Ferry Prins leads the [Photonic Nanomaterials & Devices group at the Condensed Matter Physics Center (IFIMAC) of the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid](https://portalcientifico.uam.es/en/ipublic/researcher/279572). He founded the group at ETH Zurich in 2015 and moved it to IFIMAC in 2017.

Ferry Prins curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from July 12 to July 18 2021. [Read all of Ferry's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g68).
