## About the guest

Johanna K. Jochum is an Instrument Scientist at the Research Neutron Source Heinz Maier-Leibnitz, Germany, working on RESEDA, a neutron resonant spin-echo spectrometer for measuring dynamic processes in magnetic materials. She studied physics at ETH Zurich, where her master's thesis was on iron pnictide superconductors.

Johanna K. Jochum curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from April 26 to May 2 2021. [Read all of Johanna's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g61).
