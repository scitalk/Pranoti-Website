## About the guest

Benjamin Eggleton was Director of Sydney Nano when we recorded this episode. He is now [Pro Vice-Chancellor (Research) at the University of Sydney](https://profiles.sydney.edu.au/benjamin.eggleton), where he leads the university's research operations. He is also a Professor in the School of Physics, leading an experimental photonics group, and a founding Co-Director of the NSW Smart Sensing Network.

Benjamin Eggleton curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from June 28 to July 4 2020. [Read all of Benjamin's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g32).
