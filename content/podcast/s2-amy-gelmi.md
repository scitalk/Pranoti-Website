## About the guest

Amy Gelmi was a Vice Chancellor's Research Fellow at RMIT University when we recorded this episode. She is now a [Senior Lecturer in the School of Science at RMIT University](https://www.rmit.edu.au/profiles/s/amy-gelmi), working in biomaterials, tissue engineering and nanoscale characterisation.

Amy Gelmi curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from February 29 to March 7 2020. [Read all of Amy's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g20).
