## About the guest

Jarrett Dillenburger is a Graduate Researcher at the University of Pennsylvania, USA, exploring the chemistry of layered inorganic solids and the nanosheets made from them, including exfoliation, functionalisation and thermochemistry. A favourite project is Mission Material Science, a volunteer outreach programme. Jarrett studied at Denison University.

Jarrett Dillenburger curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from April 11 to April 17 2022. [Read all of Jarrett's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g88).
