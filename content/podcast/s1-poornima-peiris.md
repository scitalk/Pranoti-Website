## About the guest

Poornima Peiris is a Student and Researcher at Harvard University, USA, working with a nanophotonics group on the absorption of nanomaterials, multispectral analysis, and nanostructured semiconductors and metallic nanowires, used to make electromechanical and photonic sensors for precision medicine, agriculture and environmental monitoring.

Poornima Peiris curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 11 to November 16 2019. [Read all of Poornima's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g7).
