## About the guest

Jarriaun Streets is a Senior Biological Scientist at the University of South Florida, USA, and Co-founder of Black in Nanotechnology. The master's research in Pharmaceutical Nanotechnology at USF used MPEG-PCL micelles for sustained drug delivery to the back of the eye, to improve treatment of age-related macular degeneration.

Jarriaun Streets curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from December 7 to December 13 2020. [Read all of Jarriaun's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g49).
