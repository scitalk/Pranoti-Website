## About the guest

Luis R. De Jesús is an Assistant Professor at the University at Buffalo – SUNY, USA, studying how phase transitions happen in solids and how bonds change, to improve how materials work, including why Li-ion batteries lose charge, charge slowly or stop working. He studied chemistry at the University of Puerto Rico at Cayey.

Luis R. De Jesús curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from June 27 to July 3 2022. [Read all of Luis's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g95).
