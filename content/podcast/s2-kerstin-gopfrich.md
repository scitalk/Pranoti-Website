## About the guest

Kerstin Göpfrich was a Max Planck Research Group Leader in Heidelberg when we recorded this episode. She is now a [professor at the Center for Molecular Biology of Heidelberg University (ZMBH)](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/de/newsroom/fuenf-erc-starting-grants-gehen-an-die-universitaet-heidelberg). Göpfrich's ERC project ENSYNC uses DNA and RNA origami to build a prototype synthetic cell that can evolve.

Kerstin Göpfrich curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from January 12 to January 18 2020. [Read all of Kerstin's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g14).
