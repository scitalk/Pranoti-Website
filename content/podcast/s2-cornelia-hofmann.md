## About the guest

Cornelia Hofmann was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at University College London when we recorded this episode. She is now a [Lecturer for Physics and Mathematics at the ZHAW School of Life Sciences and Facility Management](https://www.zhaw.ch/en/about-us/person/homc) in Switzerland, and part of the Simulation & Optimization Research Group at the Institute of Computational Life Sciences. Her work there covers modelling, simulation and optimisation of complex systems in the life sciences.

Cornelia Hofmann curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 9 to November 15 2020. [Read all of Cornelia's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g45).
