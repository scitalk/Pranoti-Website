## About the guest

Sebastian F. Maehrlein was a Group Leader at the Fritz Haber Institute of the Max Planck Society when we recorded this episode. Since June 2024 he has been [professor of high-field terahertz physics at the Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf (HZDR)](https://www.hzdr.de/db/Cms?pOid=72114), in a joint appointment with TU Dresden, where he heads the High-Field THz-driven Phenomena group at the Institute of Radiation Physics.

Sebastian F. Maehrlein curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from June 28 to July 4 2021. [Read all of Sebastian's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g66).
