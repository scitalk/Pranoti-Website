## About the guest

Peter Sherrell was a Research Fellow at the University of Melbourne when we recorded this episode. He is now a [Senior Vice-Chancellor's Research Fellow in Applied Chemistry and Environmental Science at RMIT University](https://www.rmit.edu.au/profiles/s/peter-sherrell). His research captures ambient energy from the environment, such as motion and heat, to drive green and sustainable chemistry and efficient catalysis.

Peter Sherrell curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from October 26 to November 1 2020. [Read all of Peter's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g44).
