## About the guest

Anna Piras was a PhD student at the Heidelberg Institute for Theoretical Studies when we recorded this episode. She completed her PhD in March 2024 and, according to [her CV](https://alumni2025.h-its.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/25/2025/03/CV_Anna_Piras.pdf), has since worked as a research associate at the Institute for the Chemistry of Organometallic Compounds of Italy's National Research Council (CNR-ICCOM) in Pisa, on materials for recovering and storing thermal energy.

Anna Piras curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from February 20 to February 28 2021. [Read all of Anna's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g54).
