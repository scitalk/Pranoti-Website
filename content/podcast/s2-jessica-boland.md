## About the guest

Jessica Boland was a Lecturer at the University of Manchester when we recorded this episode. Since 2024 she has been a [Senior Lecturer in Functional Materials and Devices in the Department of Materials at the University of Manchester](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/persons/jessica.boland/), and a member of the Photonic Materials and Devices Group in the Photon Science Institute. Her research focuses on non-destructive optoelectronic characterisation of low-dimensional materials for devices.

Jessica Boland curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from August 3 to August 9 2020. [Read all of Jessica's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g35).
