## About the guest

Michele Celebrano is an [Associate Professor in the Physics Department at Politecnico di Milano](https://www.fisi.polimi.it/en/staff/michele.celebrano), where Michele leads the sNOm Lab (supra Nano-Optics Milano). The lab builds nonlinear nanoantennas and metasurfaces to control light at the nanoscale, for sensing, optical logic and terahertz generation.

Michele Celebrano curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from October 7 to October 12 2019. [Read all of Michele's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g2).
