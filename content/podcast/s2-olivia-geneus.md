## About the guest

Olivia Geneus is a PhD Candidate at the University at Buffalo, USA, using nanoparticles as drug delivery systems to image and target low-oxygen, low-pH regions of tumours that resist chemo- and radiotherapy. A highlight is a nanomedicine agent that combines diagnosis and therapy, with built-in MRI contrast.

Olivia Geneus curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from September 7 to September 13 2020. [Read all of Olivia's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g39).
