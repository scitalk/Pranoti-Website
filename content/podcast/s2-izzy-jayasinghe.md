## About the guest

Izzy Jayasinghe was a UKRI Future Leaders Fellow and Lecturer at the University of Leeds when we recorded this episode. She is now an [Associate Professor at UNSW Sydney](https://www.unsw.edu.au/staff/izzy-jayasinghe), recruited in 2023 as Head of the Department of Molecular Medicine in the School of Biomedical Sciences. Her Signalling Nanodomains Laboratory combines the super-resolution microscopy tools it develops with existing technologies to study how cell signals trigger a heartbeat and drive other bodily functions.

Izzy Jayasinghe curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from February 16 to February 22 2020. [Read all of Izzy's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g19).
