## About the guest

Andrea Armani is a professor at the [University of Southern California's Viterbi School of Engineering](https://viterbi.usc.edu/directory/faculty/Armani/Andrea) and Director of the Northrop Grumman Institute for Optical Nanomaterials and Nanophotonics. Her lab develops nonlinear materials and integrated optical devices for portable disease diagnostics and telecommunications.

Andrea Armani curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from December 13 to 19 2021. [Read all of Andrea's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g82).
