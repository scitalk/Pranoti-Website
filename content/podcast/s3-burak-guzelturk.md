## About the guest

Burak Guzelturk is an Assistant Physicist at Argonne National Laboratory, USA, and a beamline scientist at the Advanced Photon Source, studying dynamic atomic-scale responses in nanomaterials. His PhD developed high-performance colloidal nanoplatelet lasers, and his postdoc made first-of-their-kind ultrafast electron diffraction measurements on nanocrystals. He started as an electrical and electronics engineer.

Burak Guzelturk curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 15 to November 21 2021. [Read all of Burak's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g80).
