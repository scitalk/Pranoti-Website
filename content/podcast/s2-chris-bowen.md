## About the guest

Chris Bowen is a Professor and [Associate Dean (Research) in the Faculty of Engineering and Design at the University of Bath](https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/persons/chris-bowen/). His research covers ferroelectric materials and composites and energy harvesting.

Chris Bowen curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from May 24 to May 30 2020. [Read all of Chris's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g29).
