## About the guest

Sean David Allen is a Postdoctoral Scholar at UCLA, USA, using nanoparticles loaded with small-molecule drugs to fight cancer by activating the immune system against tumours. The work focuses on refining new nanoparticles so they can help patients. A first-year PhD rotation in a nanoparticle lab set the direction.

Sean David Allen curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from July 12 to July 18 2020. [Read all of Sean's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g34).
