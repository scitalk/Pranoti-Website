## About the guest

Damien Leech is a Research Associate at the Centre for Fine Print Research, University of the West of England, UK, helping to revive forgotten 19th-century print practices and exploring print technology from 3D printing to screen printing, for uses from sensors to art. Recent work tracked the changing colour tones in the Woodburytype process, which layers pigmented gelatin films into full-colour images.

Damien Leech curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from May 3 to May 9 2020. [Read all of Damien's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g26).
