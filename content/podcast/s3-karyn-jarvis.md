## About the guest

Karyn Jarvis is a Senior Research Engineer at Swinburne University of Technology, Australia, working on surface modification and thin film deposition: nanometre-thick coatings that give surfaces the properties needed. A first postdoc project, with another university and a water company, worked on improving water quality.

Karyn Jarvis curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from August 22 to August 29 2021. [Read all of Karyn's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g73).
