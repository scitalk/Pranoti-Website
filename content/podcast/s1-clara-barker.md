## About the guest

Clara M. Barker is the Lab Manager for the CfAS Materials department at Oxford University and Dean of Equality and Diversity at Linacre College, UK. A thin film materials scientist, she makes films often less than a micrometre thick for superconductors, solar cells, solid-state batteries and optical coatings, and once coated spinal implants to improve their biocompatibility. She trained in electronic and electrical engineering and worked in industry before moving into materials research.

Clara M. Barker curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 4 to November 9 2019. [Read all of Clara's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g6).
