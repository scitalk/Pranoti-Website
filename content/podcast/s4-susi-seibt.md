## About the guest

Susi Seibt is a Beamline Scientist on the SAXS/WAXS beamline at the Australian Synchrotron (ANSTO), using miniature, microfluidic versions of lab flasks and tubes to watch fast nanoparticle reactions, following nucleation and growth at almost nanosecond resolution. She studied chemistry and polymer science in Bavaria, Germany, and first came to Melbourne on a DAAD research stay during her PhD.

Susi Seibt curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from June 13 to June 26 2022. [Read all of Susi's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g94).
