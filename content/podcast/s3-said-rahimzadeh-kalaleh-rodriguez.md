## About the guest

Said Rahimzadeh-Kalaleh Rodriguez is a tenure-track Scientific Group Leader at AMOLF, the Netherlands, studying light at the nanoscale, its interaction with matter, phenomena such as superfluidity of light, and how noise can benefit nanoscale optical systems. A recent group paper showed how to turn noise into an advantage for signal detection.

Said Rahimzadeh-Kalaleh Rodriguez curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from August 9 to August 15 2021. [Read all of Said's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g71).
