## About the guest

William Brant is an Assistant Professor at Uppsala University, Sweden, using X-rays and neutrons to see inside batteries and understand how electrochemically active materials form and change. With colleagues, he developed a low-cost synthesis of Prussian white, a cathode material for sodium batteries, patented it and created a spin-off company.

William Brant curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from June 21 to 27 2021. [Read all of William's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g65).
