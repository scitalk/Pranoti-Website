## About the guest

Tiffany Harte is a [Senior Research Associate in the Many-Body Quantum Dynamics group at the University of Cambridge](https://www.manybody.phy.cam.ac.uk/staff/dr-tiffany-harte). She is developing a new detector for dark matter and gravitational waves with the AION consortium, based on atom interferometry with ultracold strontium.

Tiffany Harte curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 1 to 7 2021. [Read all of Tiffany's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g78).
