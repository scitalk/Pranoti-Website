## About the guest

Reza Rouzegar is a PhD student at Freie Universität Berlin, Germany, working on spintronics, using the spin of electrons as well as their charge to carry information, and on terahertz spectroscopy. Reza started in electrical engineering, working on metasurfaces during his bachelor's.

Reza Rouzegar curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from May 1 to May 7 2022. [Read all of Reza's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g90).
