## About the guest

Haley Harrison is a Doctoral Candidate in Nanoscience at The University of North Carolina at Greensboro, USA, working to make boron nitride nanotubes compatible with high-temperature structural materials, through surface modification and aerogels, lightweight materials that insulate exceptionally well thanks to their nanoscale pores.

Haley Harrison curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from July 5 to July 11 2020. [Read all of Haley's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g33).
