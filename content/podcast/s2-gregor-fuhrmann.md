## About the guest

Gregor Fuhrmann led a junior research group at the Helmholtz Institute for Pharmaceutical Research Saarland when we recorded this episode. Since 2021 he has held the [Chair of Pharmaceutical Biology at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU)](https://www.pharmbio.nat.fau.de/staff/). He holds an ERC Starting Grant (2020), and his research looks for new biogenic routes against infectious and inflammatory diseases and characterises new natural products.

Gregor Fuhrmann curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from April 5 to April 11 2020. [Read all of Gregor's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g23).
