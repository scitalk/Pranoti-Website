## About the guest

Alexandra Brumberg was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of California, Berkeley when we recorded this episode. She is now an [Assistant Professor in the Department of Chemistry at Drexel University](https://drexel.edu/coas/faculty-research/faculty-directory/chemistry/brumberg-alexandra/). Her group links structural changes in materials, such as crystallisation, self-assembly and phase transitions, with their electronic dynamics and optoelectronic properties, using in situ methods and ultrafast characterisation.

Alexandra Brumberg curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from June 6 to June 12 2022. [Read all of Alexandra's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g93).
