## About the guest

Hortense Le Ferrand was an Assistant Professor at Nanyang Technological University when we recorded this episode. She is now an [Associate Professor in NTU's School of Materials Science and Engineering and School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-hub/news/detail/talented-research-visionaries-drawn-to-ntu), where she develops bioinspired materials for sustainable manufacturing. One recent example is a mycelium-based tile that cools buildings without consuming energy.

Hortense Le Ferrand curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 8 to 14 2021. [Read all of Hortense's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g79).
