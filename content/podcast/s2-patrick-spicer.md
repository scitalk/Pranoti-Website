## About the guest

Patrick Spicer is an [Associate Professor in the School of Chemical Engineering at UNSW Sydney](https://www.unsw.edu.au/staff/patrick-spicer), where he is Research Director for Chemical Engineering and leads the Chemical Product Engineering stream and the Fluids Foundry makerspace. He directs the Complex Fluids group, which works with industry and academic partners to design smart fluids whose flow behaviour is linked directly to product and material performance.

Patrick Spicer curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from June 14 to June 20 2020. [Read all of Patrick's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g31).
