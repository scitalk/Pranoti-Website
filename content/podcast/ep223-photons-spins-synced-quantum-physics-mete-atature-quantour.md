## Photons & Spins Synced, Quantum Physics, Mete Atature, QuanTour

## In Other Words

Demonstrating that a single photon from a semiconductor quantum dot can be quadrature-squeezed, its electric-field noise dropping below vacuum, 'below the level of nothingness', a property textbooks said was too hard to observe.

## Key Takeaways

- Professor at University of Cambridge studying light–matter interaction at the quantum level in semiconductors, diamond and 2D materials, for quantum networks and quantum sensors.
- Adding the electron spin to the single photon opens a new dimension: photon–spin entanglement lets you distribute entanglement between distant nodes and use quantum dots as quantum repeaters (no amplifiers, per no-cloning).
- No single material wins: quantum dots are the best single-photon source, diamond the best spin/memory, hBN is temperature-agnostic (good for space).
- Nanodiamond NV-centre sensors can go inside living cells (even C. elegans) to non-destructively map magnetic field, pH, temperature and viscosity at the nanoscale, a surprise life-sciences application.
- Career: Turkey → Boston → ETH Zurich → Cambridge; champions researcher mobility. Wishes: keep the same calibre of team, unallocated thinking time, and steady baseline funding.
