## About the guest

Anna Ploszajski is a [materials scientist, storyteller, author, podcaster, speaker, presenter and trainer](https://www.annaploszajski.com/about) based in London. She did her doctorate at UCL on hydrogen storage materials and wrote her thesis as a story.

Anna Ploszajski curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from January 11 to January 17 2021. [Read all of Anna's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g51).
