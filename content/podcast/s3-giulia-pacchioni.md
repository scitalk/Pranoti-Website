## About the guest

Giulia Pacchioni was a Senior Editor at Nature Reviews Materials when we recorded this episode. She is now the journal's [Chief Editor](https://www.nature.com/natrevmats/editors), based at Springer Nature in London.

Giulia Pacchioni curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from October 25 to 31 2021. [Read all of Giulia's tweets from that week](/realsci-nano-tweets/#g77).
