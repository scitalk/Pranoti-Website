In this solo episode, Pranoti Kshirsagar shares the three foundational pillars of a successful outreach strategy for a research project — drawn from real work on the QuanTour campaign, a year-long global science outreach initiative following a quantum light source across 12 labs in 12 months.

**Pillar 1: Define and align**
Before creating any content, ask: who is this for, and why now? For QuanTour, this meant building an audience curious about quantum science beyond just specialists, spotlighting the scientists and labs involved, and partnering with science societies and universities to amplify reach and credibility.

**Pillar 2: Craft the message**
Once you know your audience, articulate the story. QuanTour's narrative — *12 months, 12 labs, one source* — was simple but powerful. It centred both the technology and the human stories driving quantum research forward.

**Pillar 3: Choose the channels and formats**
The same story can reach different audiences in different ways. QuanTour used podcast episodes, videos, visuals, press coverage, social media, and physical media like posters and stickers — all without diluting the core message.

These pillars apply whether you're building a one-day event campaign or a year-long dissemination project. The framework — audience, message, format — brings clarity and direction to any science communication or outreach effort.
