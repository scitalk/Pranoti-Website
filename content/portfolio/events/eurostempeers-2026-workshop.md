A hands-on workshop on using generative AI tools to strengthen grant applications and tailor CVs to specific funding calls — practical workflows rather than theory, drawn from Pranoti's work helping researchers submit European proposals worth more than €32 million.

The session focused on where AI genuinely speeds up proposal writing (structuring arguments, tightening language, adapting a CV to a specific call's priorities) versus where it can introduce risk if used carelessly, giving attendees a workflow they could take straight into their next grant application.
