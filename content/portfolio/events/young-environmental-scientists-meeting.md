At the YES Meeting 2023 in Landau, Pranoti delivered both a Personal Branding Workshop and a Science Communication Training session for early-career environmental scientists.

> "I found the workshop insightful and inspiring to further develop my own personal brand as a scientist on professional social media. Dr Kshirsagar graciously shared her own tips and tricks to enhance visibility, increase engagement and grow followers. I would recommend the workshop if you are looking for structure of ways to 'shine' in the digital world!"
>
> — **Prof. Athina Vidaki**, Maastricht University

> "It was an opportunity to attend the science communication training session given by Dr. Pranoti. I learnt a lot on how to communicate science in creative ways using social media and podcasts. Most importantly, Dr Pranoti delivered an outstanding session by explaining in simple terms how to communicate science with clear examples. This has inspired me to imagine a Podcast series of my own."
>
> — **Dr. Fola Ogungbemi**, Regulatory Scientist / Ecotoxicologist
