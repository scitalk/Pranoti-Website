Pranoti was invited to share her career journey with the students of VigyanShaala's Kalpana-She for STEM program — a mentorship cohort supporting female students pursuing STEM degrees across India.

> "Dr. Pranoti Kshirsagar was invited to share her journey with the students of Kalpana-She for STEM program. Her rich and diverse experience inspired the young aspiring minds. Her journey from academia to the founder of 'The Science Talk', left a lasting impression on our students. Dr. Kshirsagar's insights into the challenges and triumphs of a career in STEM, coupled with her dedication to science communication, provided our students with valuable lessons. We are deeply grateful for her time and for sharing her remarkable story."
>
> — **Dr. Tripti Joshi**, Director, VigyanShaala International
