At HdM Quantum Day 2026, Pranoti spoke about the strategic partnership between **QuanTour** and **The Science Talk**, and how partnerships like this can amplify research impact. As communication lead for the QuanTour project — winner of the 2025 Lise Meitner Medal and Prize from the Institute of Physics for innovative public engagement across the UK and Europe — she brought a practitioner's view of what makes science communication partnerships actually work.

The talk was followed by a panel discussion on the role of science communication in quantum research, including questions on funding, generative AI's impact on digital SciComm, and why partnering with a dedicated communicator (rather than relying solely on an institution's press office) changes outcomes for research visibility.
