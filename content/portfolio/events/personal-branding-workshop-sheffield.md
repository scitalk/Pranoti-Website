## Personal Branding for Scientists: Your Science, Your Story

**2-Hour Interactive Workshop**, delivered in person at the University of Sheffield for Dr Alex Ramadan's research group.

Building a confident online presence is essential for early and mid-career researchers, but many struggle with where to start and how to stand out without feeling like they're "selling themselves." This hands-on workshop guided participants through creating an authentic personal brand that opens doors to collaborations, speaking opportunities, and career advancement — whether they stay in academia or transition elsewhere.

The biggest challenge many early and mid-career researchers face isn't a lack of intelligence or hard work — it's a lack of strategy and confidence in presenting themselves. This workshop helped attendees articulate their value beyond research outputs and communicate it effectively in digital spaces.

**What participants walked away with:**

1. **Clear Career Goals Framework** — a structured approach to defining what they actually want from their career (academia, industry, entrepreneurship, advocacy) and how their personal brand should support those specific goals.
2. **Personal Brand Statement** — a clear, compelling statement capturing who they are, what they do, and what makes them unique — ready to use across all platforms.
3. **Platform Selection Strategy** — a decision framework for choosing which digital platform (LinkedIn, academic profiles, personal website) best serves their career goals.
4. **AI Integration Toolkit** — practical prompts and workflows for using AI tools to draft posts, refine messaging, and maintain consistency without losing an authentic voice.
5. **48-Hour Action Plan** — concrete, achievable next steps to start building a digital presence immediately after the workshop.
