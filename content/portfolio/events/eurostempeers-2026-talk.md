A short talk on the EU AI Act aimed at researchers and early-career scientists navigating what the regulation actually requires of them — cutting through the legal language to explain the practical compliance questions that come up when adopting AI tools in a research setting.

Drawing on her specialist knowledge of the EU AI Act and EU Digital Act, and having worked on AI products since 2019, Pranoti focused on what's genuinely relevant for a working scientist rather than a full legal breakdown: which AI use cases carry which obligations, and how to evaluate tools for compliance before adopting them in a lab or research group.
