faq_heading: "Questions we get asked"
faq:
  - q: "Do we need a studio, a microphone, or any equipment?"
    a: "No. We record one remote session and capture broadcast-quality audio and video from our side. You need a quiet room and an internet connection. No travel, no hire, no software to buy."
  - q: "How fast can we go from recording to release?"
    a: "Two weeks, at the quickest. That covers editing, artwork, show notes, your approval round and scheduling. If you are working to a conference date, an embargo or a project milestone, tell us on the scoping call and we will plan the recording backwards from it."
  - q: "Can we use the episode on our own channels?"
    a: "Yes. Share it, embed it on your project or institute website, and put it in your reporting. The Science Talk holds the copyright in the episode, so the files stay ours and are not re-licensed or resold, but you are free to use and point to the published episode."
  - q: "Can we put two people from our project on one episode?"
    a: "Often, yes, but it changes the recording and the edit, so it is priced separately. Raise it on the scoping call and we will tell you whether the format works for your two speakers and what it costs."
  - q: "Do you record in languages other than English?"
    a: "Episodes are recorded and produced in English as standard. A version in another language is possible at additional cost — for example, the episode with Prof. Humeyra Caglayan on snapshot 3D microscopy is published in English and in a [Finnish version](https://youtu.be/95WDFwisWmY). If your audience or your funder needs a second language, ask before we record."
  - q: "Do we see the episode before it goes public?"
    a: "Yes. Nothing is published until you have approved it. You review the edit, the show notes and the artwork, and we make changes before release day."
  - q: "Who decides what the episode is about?"
    a: "You do, on the scoping call. We agree the angle, the audience and the key messages first, and you get a short brief before recording so there are no surprises in the room."
  - q: "Where does the episode actually get published?"
    a: "The audio goes out on Under the Microscope, which is distributed on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, Castbox, Goodpods and any app that reads the public RSS feed. The video goes on The Science Talk YouTube channel. Everything is free to access from day one — no paywall, no login."
  - q: "What does the promotion actually involve?"
    a: "You get a dated campaign calendar built around your project, not a generic checklist. Release day carries the announcement post and custom graphic on LinkedIn and Instagram, tagging your institution, your funders and your project partners by name. Over the following week, short-form clips roll out across LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube on a staggered schedule, plus a feature in The Science Talk newsletter and a personal post from Pranoti. Larger packages bring the episode back for further promotion rounds months later."
  - q: "Do we find out how it performed?"
    a: "Yes. You get reach reported per platform and per piece of content — video views and post impressions on LinkedIn, views on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and podcast plays — with the metric named so the numbers are comparable rather than pooled into one flattering figure."
  - q: "We have a dissemination work package. Does this fit?"
    a: "It is built for it. The outputs are the kind reporting asks for: a public, permanently accessible audio and video episode, a dated record of every promotional post, and per-platform reach figures you can cite directly."
  - q: "We are not a household name. Will anyone actually hear it?"
    a: "Your episode lands in an established feed, not a new one. Under the Microscope has run for eight seasons and over 300 episodes, with guests from 40 countries, and it already has subscribers who listen because of the show rather than the guest."
  - q: "How does billing work?"
    a: "We invoice in full on booking, once you have confirmed the package and we have a recording date in the diary. All prices are quoted excluding VAT. If your institute needs a purchase order number, a quotation in a particular format, or specific reference details on the invoice, send them over and we will issue it accordingly."
