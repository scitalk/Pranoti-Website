The ERC Consolidator Grant supports researchers who defended their PhD more than 5 and up to 15 years ago and are consolidating their own independent research team. The eligibility window can be extended for parental leave, long-term illness, national service and similar documented circumstances. It sits between the Starting Grant and the Advanced Grant, and it is where many researchers make the step from promising to established.

This page lists the funded projects for every Consolidator Grant call from 2016 through 2025 — the researcher, host institution, country, research domain, and a direct link to the project record on CORDIS. Use the filters to narrow by call year or by domain (Physical Sciences & Engineering, Life Sciences, Social Sciences & Humanities).

The 2016–2020 calls ran under Horizon 2020; from 2021 onward, under Horizon Europe. Full eligibility rules, extensions, grant amounts and durations are set out in the [ERC Work Programme](https://erc.europa.eu/funding/erc-work-programme) and on [the ERC's own site](https://erc.europa.eu/apply-grant/consolidator-grant).
