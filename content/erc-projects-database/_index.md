This is a browsable record of every grant the European Research Council has funded from 2016 to 2025 — 11,953 projects across Starting, Consolidator, Advanced, Synergy and Proof of Concept grants. For each one you get the researcher, host institution, country, research domain, call year, and a direct link to the project record on CORDIS. Filter by scheme, year, or domain to see who has been funded, where, and for what. The data comes from the European Commission's own [ERC dashboard](https://dashboard.tech.ec.europa.eu/qs_digit_dashboard_mt/public/sense/app/c140622a-87e0-412e-8b29-9b5ddd857e13), exported on 26 August 2026.

If you are preparing an application yourself, the scheme pages below carry funded-vs-submitted success rates for every call, and each links to the matching resource kit.
