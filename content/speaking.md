## About Pranoti

Pranoti Kshirsagar is a science communication expert and EIC Partner who supports over 7,000 innovations across European countries through the European Innovation Council. Her work spans strategic consulting for researchers and innovation-driven companies, podcast production, and educational content creation around quantum science and technology.

Through The Science Talk, Pranoti runs workshops and consulting services that help researchers communicate effectively, understand AI policy requirements, and translate scientific concepts for broader audiences. She developed the AI Academy by The Science Talk, providing comprehensive compliance resources for researchers using GenAI tools.

Pranoti operates across the European research ecosystem, with deep expertise in Horizon Europe guidelines, institutional AI policies, and innovation funding mechanisms. She's based in Germany and regularly speaks at virtual and in-person events across Europe.
