RealSci_Nano was a rotation curation account. Each week, a new materials or nanoscientist took over the account and shared their research, their life in the lab, and their questions. It ran alongside the Under the Microscope podcast, and many curators were also podcast guests.

In 2024 I closed shop on Twitter/X and moved to Bluesky, so the account now lives on as Under the Microscope. This page keeps every post the curators wrote, grouped by year and curator, so the work doesn't disappear with the platform. For the full story, see the [RealSci_Nano project page](/projects/realsci-nano/).
