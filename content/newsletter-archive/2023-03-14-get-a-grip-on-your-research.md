# Are you thinking about doing a PhD, currently a PhD candidate, or early career researcher?

The PhD Talk podcast is here for you! Co-hosts [Eva Lantsoght](https://www.evalantsoght.com/about-me.html), civil engineering professor, blogger, and author, and Rico Massa, PhD candidate in civil engineering, discuss research mechanics, PhD life, and the tools for doing research. Tune in each week for strategies to help you succeed in your PhD trajectory.

**Researcher Workbook: Get a grip on your research**

Get grip on your research by working through the 24 exercises provided in this book. You will learn how to manage your workload, your time, your energy, your surroundings, and your brand. This workbook is suitable for researchers at all stages of their careers: PhD candidates, post-doctoral researchers, tenure track faculty, as well as tenured professors.
