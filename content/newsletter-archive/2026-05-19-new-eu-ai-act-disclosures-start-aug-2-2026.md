Are you ready for Article 50?

Hello helllooo,

The EU AI Act’s transparency obligations under Article 50 apply from **2 August 2026** — and if you publish AI-generated content professionally, you have specific disclosure duties that go well beyond adding a footnote to your terms and conditions.

For the past two years I've been evaluating different AI tools and I just winged it when it came to disclosures. And so, honestly, I'm very happy to see these guidelines which will soon be regulations.

Before we move on...

A special welcome to new joiners of this newsletter. 🤗  
My name is Pranoti and I'm the founder of [The Science Talk](https://thesciencetalk.com/).  
My mission is to empower and enable scientists to share their stories in digital spaces with confidence and ease. 🧡

**What's in scope:** AI-generated or manipulated text published on matters of public interest (research, health, policy, environment, science). This includes blog posts, social media, video scripts, and podcast show notes.

**Where disclosures must appear:** At the **start** of your content — before the first paragraph, at the top of captions, or as an on-screen overlay. End-of-post disclaimers and terms-and-conditions links don't meet the standard.

**What qualifies as editorial review:** Substantive human review by someone with editorial responsibility — not spell-checks or light read-throughs.

**Your next step:** Build disclosure statements into your publishing templates now, before the deadline.
