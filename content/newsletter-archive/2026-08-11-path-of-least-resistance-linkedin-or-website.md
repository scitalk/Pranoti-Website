LinkedIn for reach, website for record.

"I want to communicate my research, what is a better option - group website or LinkedIn?"

90% of my clients ask me this question in my personal branding workshops and 1:1 consultations.

My answer: it depends on your career stage.

A special welcome to new joiners of this newsletter. 🤗  
My name is Pranoti and I am the founder of [The Science Talk](https://thesciencetalk.com/).  
My mission is to empower and enable scientists to share their stories in digital spaces with confidence and ease. 🧡

I understand where this question is coming from.

I know you are already juggling 10 different things at the same time - supervising students, manuscripts, running your lab, filing reimbursement for a conference you attended 3 months ago.

You are looking for the most effective option.

You are hoping for that ONE thing which will get you the visibility by following the path of least resistance.

And this is exactly why I added "Platform selection" module in my workshop **Personal Branding for Scientists in the age of AI**.

[Attend Personal Branding workshop on Aug 25](https://thesciencetalk.com/online-course-personal-branding-for-scientists-in-the-age-of-ai/)

To answer your question, LinkedIn or group website, think about it this way👇

**LinkedIn = Visibility.**  
**Website = Track record.**

For **early career researchers** - PhDs, postdocs, group leaders with team of 2 scientists - **LinkedIn** should be your priority 1.

For **mid career researchers** - group leaders with more than 2 scientists - **Group website** should be your priority 1.
