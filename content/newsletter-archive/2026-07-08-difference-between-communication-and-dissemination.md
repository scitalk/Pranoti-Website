relevant for you and your research proposals.

Communication and dissemination aren't the same thing — and treating them separately is **what makes your research stand out.**

Communication builds your credibility over time. It's broad and ongoing — papers, conference talks, the general visibility you build just by doing good research and showing up in your field.

Dissemination works differently. It's the deliberate, targeted transfer of your research to specific audiences who can use it — think podcasts, videos, webinars, press releases.

Done well, dissemination is what opens the door to new collaborations, gets you on recruiter's radar, and helps you attract strong talent to your group.

Both matter, and both are worth planning for — they just run on different timelines, different skills, and often different people executing them.

Try this: Take one upcoming project or proposal.  
Ask yourself: who's my dissemination audience, and what's the one channel that actually reaches them?
