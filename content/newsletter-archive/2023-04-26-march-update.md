## SciArt of Apr 2023

Virtual section through the eye of a developing zebrafish. The cells you see here are neurons of the eye that are important for signal processing in the eye as they relay visual information to the visual nerve, that leads into the brain. Did you know that there are five different neuron types in the eye? And each one has tens-to-hundreds of subtypes? Science is still far from understanding how all these cells work together.
