Hellooo!

The MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship results reopened research funding debate.

**﻿﻿﻿17,066 proposals** submitted. **1,610 funded.**
**9.6% success rate.**

Scientists shared their opinions on LinkedIn and engaged in intense
discussions. Some shared concerns over the alarmingly low success rate. Others
said stop complaining & move on.

I calculated the economic impact of the "invisible & unpaid" work 
scientists do in order to get funding. Full breakdown [in this
blogpost](https://thesciencetalk.com/news/blog-perspectives/hidden-cost-msca-postdoctoral-fellowship-2025/) and on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/pranoti-kshirsagar_scientists-did-469-million-worth-of-work-activity-7427737105050705920-QWvl?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAAqyA_0Bpiox4m_8mQfvft7V9nsN1ELh1ho) (49 comments, 196
reactions). The comments are spicy so brace yourself.

This isn't a criticism of MSCA. But the structural challenge is real. While the
system evolves, the strongest move is making your next application as strategic
and efficient as possible — starting well before you write a single word.
