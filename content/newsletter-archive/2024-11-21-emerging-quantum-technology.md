We’re thrilled to bring you the latest episode of *Under the Microscope*! This week, **Dr. Pranoti Kshirsagar** sits down with the incredible **Brian Gerardot**, Professor at Heriot-Watt University, to dive into his pioneering work in **quantum devices** and **emerging technologies**. If you’re curious about how cutting-edge quantum materials are shaping the future, this is a must-listen! 🎧✨

✨ **Episode Highlights**:

🛠️ **Quantum Device Innovation**: Discover how Brian’s lab is engineering quantum devices using strained quantum dots and Moiré superlattices.

💎 **Moiré Patterns**: Learn how twisting 2D materials creates fascinating properties like magnetism and quantum light sources.

🌍 **QuanTour Project**: Hear about the collaborative outreach project connecting labs across Europe to showcase advancements in quantum technology.

🚀 **Challenges & Opportunities**: Gain insights into scaling quantum technologies for real-world applications.
