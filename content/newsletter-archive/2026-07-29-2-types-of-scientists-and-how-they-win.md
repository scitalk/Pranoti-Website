research grants and awards.

Over the years, I've worked with and interviewed hundreds of scientists. And somewhere along the way, I started noticing a pattern - there are two types of scientists when it comes to communicating impact of their research.

**Type 1** publishes in journals and goes to conferences. Everything beyond that is done half heartedly. Be it participating in public events liek open doors day or organising a summer school or posting on social media.

**Type 2** does all of the above too. But with intention and a clear plan. Because they really care about communicating the impact of their research beyond their collaborators. They want to nurture the next generation of scientist through their summer school.
