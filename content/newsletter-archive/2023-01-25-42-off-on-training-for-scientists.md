## SciArt of the month

Virtual section through the eye of a developing zebrafish. The cells you see here are specialized glia cells called Müller Glia, which fulfil many important functions and link to neurons and blood vessels in the eye. The image you see shows Müller Glia contacting photo receptors on the top, their interaction zone with neurons as a band in the middle, and their contact region to blood vessels at the bottom. The image was highly processed and colours show different depths of the 3D stack.
