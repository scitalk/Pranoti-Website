## **I used genAI to Submit €1M Proposals**

Last month marked the end of one of the most intense proposal seasons I've experienced—five research proposals worth over €1M, submitted across Germany, Denmark, and the European Commission. While managing this alongside running The Science Talk full-time (and battling a cold), I discovered something game-changing: the right AI workflow doesn't just save time—it transforms how you approach complex projects.

**🎯 The Reality Check**

Each funding call had its own documents, budget requirements, and application criteria. The traditional approach would have meant weeks of grinding through repetitive tasks, leaving little energy for what actually matters: strategy, storytelling, and vision.

My solution? A strategic AI-powered workflow that let me focus on the high-impact work while automating the heavy lifting.

**⚡ The 6-Tool Strategic Workflow**Here's how I used each tool strategically:

* **Claude** → Breaking the blank page with fast first drafts and initial concept development
* **ChatGPT** → Strategic work packages and understanding evaluation psychology
* **Gemini** → Literature reviews and contextual research
* **Perplexity** → General market research and competitive landscape analysis
* **Nano Banana** → Concept mockups and visual sketches for technical diagrams
* **NapkinAI** → Rapid visualization when stuck on complex concepts

The key insight? **No single tool does everything.** Success comes from knowing which tool excels where and layering them strategically.

**💡 What Actually Worked (And What Didn't)**

Here's what I learned from managing €1M worth of proposals with AI assistance:

*What eliminated the grind:* Automated literature reviews, rapid budget iterations, and visual concept development
*What stayed entirely human:* Strategic positioning, understanding funder priorities, and the critical "why this project matters" narrative
*The surprise win:* AI helped me spot gaps in logic and strengthen weak arguments I might have missed

**📊 The Full Breakdown**

I've documented the complete workflow—specific tools, exact prompts, what worked, what failed, and real examples from each proposal—because I believe this approach could transform how researchers tackle large-scale applications.

The methodology isn't just about speed; it's about maintaining quality while scaling your capacity to pursue ambitious projects.
