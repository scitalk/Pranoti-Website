on Under the Microscope podcast.

Proposal writing is exhausting.

Specially at the beginning of your career as an independent researcher.

If you're an early career scientist based in Europe then you're probably applying for the ERC Starting Grant 2027.

Now...ERC grants are highly competitive and application process can be intimidating for scientists.

Imposter syndrome is at all time high.

Am I good enough😬 How do I even talk about my work 😳 What if my proposal isn't ambitious enough🫣

When I'm overwhelmed and doubting myself, listening with someone who has been through the grind really helps!

This is exactly what I have for you today.

A special welcome to new joiners of this newsletter. 🤗  
My name is Pranoti and I am the founder of [The Science Talk](https://thesciencetalk.com/).  
My mission is to empower and enable scientists to share their stories in digital spaces with confidence and ease. 🧡

Over the years, I've had the privilege of interviewing **11 ERC Starting grant winners** on my podcast - Under the Microscope🎙️

On each episode of my podcast, the guest scientist speaks very candidly about their research, their career journey, and their three wishes for science community. You get to see the human side of scientists.🧡

ICYMI, [Under the Microscope](https://thesciencetalk.com/under-the-microscope/) is an independently run self-financed podcast where we spotlight materials and nanoscience. As the host and executive producer I'm very happy and proud to share that we're in the 8th season and we've produced 300+ episodes featuring scientists from 40 countries.🥳

[Read blogpost about 11 ERC Starting Grant winners](https://thesciencetalk.com/news/erc-starting-grant-winners-under-the-microscope-podcast/)

Typical podcast conversation flow:

1. Please explain your research in simple words (max 5 lines)
2. Could you share your career journey with us so far?
3. Which research project are you most proud of and could you explain it in simple words in the section we call #InOtherwords?
4. Other than research, what else do like about your life a scientist?
5. If you had 3 wishes to improve your research experience, what would you ask for (not promising anything here!)?
6. How are you and your institute adopting AI?
