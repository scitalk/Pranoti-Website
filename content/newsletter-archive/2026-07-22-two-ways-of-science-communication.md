and why human angle always wins!

When creating content for science communication, you generally have two options:

1. **The Science Angle:** You talk strictly about the research projects—focusing on scope, technical results, and future outlooks.
2. **The Human Angle:** You focus on the scientist’s journey—their background, how they think, and the human story behind the discoveries.

At ***Under the Microscope\****, we take the second, human angle. We put the scientist under the microscope, and through their personal and professional journey, we spotlight the incredible world of materials and nanoscience.

#### Inside the Latest Episode: Meet Dr. Anna Sandak

In our latest episode, this human-first approach brings us a fascinating conversation with **Dr. Anna Sandak**, Head of the Materials Group at the University of Primorska.

Rather than just listing research data, I sat down with Anna to explore:

* **From biology to building facades:** How her background in biology inspired her to engineer living fungal coatings for architecture.
* **Living functionality:** Why these coatings can self-heal scratches, absorb air pollutants, and use melanin to provide UV protection.
* **Grants & scale:** The distinction between her **ERC Consolidator grant (ARCHI-SKIN)** and her commercialisation-focused **EIC Pathfinder project (REMEDY)**.
* **"Living Tattoos":** How high-resolution bio-printing combines artistic decoration with functional living microbial consortia on buildings.
* **Microgravity tests:** Why her lab is sending fungal biofilms into space to test their resilience under extreme conditions.
* **Adoption:** Her perspective on AI adoption for researchers ahead of the EU AI Act taking effect in August 2026.
