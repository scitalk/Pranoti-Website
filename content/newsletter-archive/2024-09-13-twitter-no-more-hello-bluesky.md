# Twitter no more. Hello Bluesky.

![](https://storage.mlcdn.com/account_image/27434/1i8hej6P1GPp3hj6mXgxmPmeQY99e3oRoKdEbuaz.png)

## RealSci\_Nano is now "Under the Microscope"

I know. What the heck, right?! 

If you’ve been following [*Under the Microscope* podcast](https://thesciencetalk.com/under-the-microscope/) since its inception six years ago, you’re probably familiar with *[Real Scientists Nano](https://twitter.com/RealSci_Nano)*, a rotation curation account that featured a new materials and nanoscientist each week. This account was an essential part of our mission to bridge the gap between groundbreaking research in the fields of materials & nanoscience and the public.

Let me tell you, this was tough decision and I have been postponing it since months. But the time has come to close shop on Twitter/X and start fresh on Bluesky. This move ensures that both the podcast and the rotation curation account are unified under one brand, making it easier for you to stay connected with the latest insights in materials and nanoscience.

**What’s Changing?**

Nothing. Well, one thing. The name of the rotation curation account is now Under the Microscope. Everything else is exactly the same. Same podcast, same concept, and the exact same team.   
I want to personally invite you to follow the *Under the Microscope* [BlueSky](https://bsky.app/profile/underthemicroscope.bsky.social) account.

More details in the latest news on The Science Talk website. Read the [blogpost](https://thesciencetalk.com/news/realscientists-nano-is-now-under-the-microscope-on-bluesky/).

If you prefer to hear my voice, listen to the announcement episode on Under the Microscope podcast which you should all be following already. So no link in the text, scroll to the bottom of this newsletter and pick your favourite podcast app. 😉
