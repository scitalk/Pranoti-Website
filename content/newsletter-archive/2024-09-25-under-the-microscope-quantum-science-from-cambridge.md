## Quantum Ceilidh: Photons & Spin in Sync

Autumn is here. It is time to cozy up and listen to your favourite podcast.

I am thrilled to share the latest episode of *Under the Microscope*! In Episode 223, I had the absolute pleasure of speaking with **Professor Mete Atature**, a leading researcher from the **University of Cambridge** and the 6th **[QuanTour](https://thesciencetalk.com/quantour/) Hero**.

In this episode, we explore the fascinating world of **quantum networks**, **secure communications**, and **quantum sensing technologies**. Mete breaks down how cutting-edge materials like **semiconductor quantum dots**, **diamonds**, and **hexagonal boron nitride** are driving the future of **quantum computing** and **sensing applications**.

🎧 **Here’s What You Can Expect from Episode 223:**

• The impact of **quantum networks** on **secure communication**.

• How **quantum dots** and **diamonds** are helping us advance **photonics**.

• The potential applications of quantum technologies in **life sciences**, with nanoscale precision and diagnostic tools.

Plus, we talk about Mete's inspiring career journey and what keeps him at the forefront of **quantum science**.
