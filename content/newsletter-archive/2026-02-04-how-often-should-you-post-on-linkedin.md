**"How often should I post?"**

I get this question in every training. My response surprises the room because they have been told to post 3-5 times per week.   

But the thing is, the algorithm doesn't care about your posting streak. It cares about whether your post is adding value to the people who read it.

A special welcome to new joiners of this newsletter. 🤗  
My name is Pranoti and I am the founder of [The Science Talk](https://thesciencetalk.com/).  
My mission is to empower and enable scientists to share their stories in digital spaces with confidence and ease. 🧡

Here's what I've learnt: **1) Strategy over frequency** - A thoughtfully crafted post every two weeks will outperform daily content that doesn't resonate. **2) Clarity over consistency** - Know exactly who you're writing for and what problem you're solving for them. Group leaders transitioning from individual contributor to strategic leader need different content than early-career researchers. Specificity creates connection. Randomness creates noise. **3) Insight over activity** - What shifts thinking? What validates a challenge they're facing? What offers genuine value? These questions matter far more than maintaining a calendar.
