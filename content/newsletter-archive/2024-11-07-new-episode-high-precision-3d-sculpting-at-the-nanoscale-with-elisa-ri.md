Hi there,

We’re thrilled to bring you the latest episode of *Under the Microscope*! This week, Dr. Pranoti Kshirsagar sits down with the incredible **Elisa Riedo**, Professor at New York University, to dive into her pioneering work in nanoscale materials and the cutting-edge **[NanoFrazor](https://nanofrazor.com/)** technology. If you’re curious about how 3D sculpting at the nanoscale is transforming fields from medicine to electronics, this is a must-listen!

**✨ Episode 226 Highlights**:

🛠️ **Revolutionary Technology**: Learn how the **[NanoFrazor](https://nanofrazor.com/)** is enabling nanoscale 3D sculpting with atomic precision, opening new doors in material science.

💎 **Diamond-Like Graphene Layers**: Discover how these structures hold potential for advanced protective materials.

🌍 **From Italy to New York**: Elisa’s inspiring journey in science—from a small Italian town to making groundbreaking discoveries in New York.

🧬 **At-Home STI Testing**: Insights into Elisa’s startup, Glaucus Technology, and its mission to develop accessible, at-home health diagnostics.
