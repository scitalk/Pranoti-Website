Hello hello,

This week I wrapped up the 2nd edition of my AI for Scientists training and I want to share my thoughts with you.

Before we get into the details, special welcome to everyone who recently joined this newsletter. My name is Pranoti and I am the founder/creator of The Science Talk. My mission is to empower and enable scientists to share their stories in digital spaces with confidence and ease.

Soo as I was saying - after training 40 scientists, 1:1 consulting for 2 clients and conversations with hundreds of scientists - I see three main stages for scientists (and anyone for that matter) in adopting AI tools in their workflows. Stages 0, 1, and 2. 👇

![](https://storage.mlcdn.com/account_image/27434/Y2zZf5jJ6ho25fWfEwb992FxmMDVO92rI7oAT5TX.png)

What's common across these three stages is that **scientists aren't resisting AI.**They're just at different stages of their journey. And each stage needs something different. **Stage #1 ne****eds** a tool overview and real use cases so they can build their own framework. **Stage #2 needs** advanced strategies to integrate AI into their actual research workflows.

Recognising which stage you are at really helps labs, institutions, and science communicators design **AI adoption strategies** that actually work.

**The question is, which stage are you at?**
