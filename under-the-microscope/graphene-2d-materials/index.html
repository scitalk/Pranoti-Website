Graphene & 2D Materials: Under the Microscope Podcast Episodes

Over the years, Under the Microscope has hosted conversations with researchers working on graphene and other atomically thin materials, from nanotunnels and strain engineering to twisted and layered 2D crystals. This page brings together 21 episodes with 12 researchers on Graphene & 2D Materials, released between 2020 and 2025.
