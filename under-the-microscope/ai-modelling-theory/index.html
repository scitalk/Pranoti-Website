AI, Modelling & Theory: Under the Microscope Podcast Episodes

Over the years, Under the Microscope has hosted conversations with researchers working on AI, computational modelling and theory in materials science, from machine learning for nanomaterials to simulations of crystals, carbon and thermodynamics. This page brings together 11 episodes with 7 researchers on AI, Modelling & Theory, released between 2020 and 2023.
