Creating New Quantum Materials Atom by Atom with Tiffany Harte

By Pranoti Kshirsagar / November 1, 2021

About the guest

Tiffany Harte is a Senior Research Associate in the Many-Body Quantum Dynamics group at the University of Cambridge. She is developing a new detector for dark matter and gravitational waves with the AION consortium, based on atom interferometry with ultracold strontium.

Tiffany Harte curated the Real Scientists Nano Twitter account (@RealSci_Nano) from November 1 to 7 2021. Read all of Tiffany’s tweets from that week.
