Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this workshop designed for?

This workshop is specifically designed for early and mid-career researchers (PhD students, postdocs, research associates, and junior faculty) who want to build their professional visibility. Whether you're planning to stay in academia, considering industry, or exploring entrepreneurship, this workshop gives you the tools to amplify your impact.

I'm not comfortable with self-promotion. Will this help?

Absolutely! This is one of the most common concerns we address. The workshop reframes personal branding as "sharing your science" rather than "selling yourself." You'll learn how to showcase your expertise through serving your community—sharing insights, helping others, and contributing value.

What makes this different from generic personal branding workshops?

Everything about this workshop is tailored specifically for scientists. The examples come from research contexts, the platforms we discuss include academic networks, and the frameworks account for the unique challenges of scientific careers.

Do I need to be tech-savvy or familiar with AI tools?

Not at all! We start from the basics and build up. The AI tools we introduce are user-friendly and require no coding or technical expertise. You'll get step-by-step guidance and ready-to-use templates.

What if I can't attend live?

All registered participants receive access to the recording for 30 days, plus all materials and templates. However, the live experience is highly recommended because of the interactive elements and real-time feedback.

How quickly will I see results?

Most participants report immediate benefits—having clarity about their brand and a concrete plan feels transformative. Visible results (increased profile views, new connections, collaboration invitations) typically start within 2-4 weeks of implementing your strategy.

Can this help with job applications?

Definitely! A clear personal brand strengthens every application. You'll know how to articulate your unique value, have a professional online presence that recruiters can find, and understand how to network strategically.
