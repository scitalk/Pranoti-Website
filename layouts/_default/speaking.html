About Pranoti

Pranoti is a speaker, trainer, and science communication strategist. Her speaking style is clear, practical, and research-backed. She has a way of taking complex ideas and making them accessible for diverse audiences.

Pranoti supports over 7,000 innovations across 16 European countries through the European Innovation Council. She runs workshops and consulting services through The Science Talk.

She is based in Germany and regularly speaks at virtual and in-person events across Europe.
