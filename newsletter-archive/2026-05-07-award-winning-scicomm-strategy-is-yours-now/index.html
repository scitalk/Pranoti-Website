Award-winning SciComm strategy is yours now

7 May 2026

and ERC reversed stricter restriction decision.

Hello helllooo,

If you think communication is difficult in a 3 partner consortium, then I have news for you.

Now multiply that number by 5.

One of my recent projects - QuanTour had 15 partners from 13 countries and I was the communication & dissemination lead.

Yep.

15 principle investigators, hard core quantum physicists with their own ideas and approach to science communication.

QuanTour was part of the UNESCO-led IYQ celebration and went on to win awards for innovative public engagement, saw over 1.3 million engagements, 30+ press releases in 9 languages, featured by 4 physics societies (German, Spanish, American, UK), I can go on and on and on. But you get the point.

None of this was accidental.

The communication, outreach, public engagement - all of it was build on three foundational pillars.

A special welcome to new joiners of this newsletter. 🤗
My name is Pranoti and I’m the founder of The Science Talk.
My mission is to empower and enable scientists to share their stories in digital spaces with confidence and ease. 🧡

The 3 foundational pillars:

Define and align – Who is this for? Why now?
Craft the message – What’s the story you’re telling?
Choose channels and formats – Where will your audience actually engage?

Same story. Different formats. No dilution.

In this solo episode I walk you through exactly how we created the communication strategy for QuanTour.
