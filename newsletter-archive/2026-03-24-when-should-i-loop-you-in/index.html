"When should I loop you in?"

Sent on 24 March 2026

A special welcome to new joiners of this newsletter. 🤗
My name is Pranoti and I am the founder of The Science Talk.
My mission is to empower and enable scientists to share their stories in digital spaces with confidence and ease. 🧡

Hello hellooo!

If you’re working on a large consortium proposal, here’s a question worth asking early: when should you bring in a science communication expert?

Most teams think about it after the grant is awarded. But by then, you’ve already locked in your budget.

The sweet spot is the budgeting stage — when you’re adding numbers to the work packages and budget tables.

By this point, you’ve done the hard thinking:
✅ Your scientific concept is solid
✅ Your consortium is formed
✅ You know the shape and scale of the project

You have everything you need to make a well-informed decision about your communication work package.

This is where I can help. I work with you to shape the communication parts of your project management work package so they fit your project’s actual scope — not a generic placeholder that gets cut later.

With the right budget secured from the start, your team can communicate your research and build your visibility from day one.

Speaking of which — if you’re currently preparing your ERC Synergy 2027 proposal, I’m running a guidance session for exactly this. Details below!

Cheers,
Pranoti

📌 8 Open Funding Calls: European Research & Innovation 🇪🇺

Deadlines are creeping up. Here’s your quick-reference list of open and upcoming funding calls — for individual scientists and research projects.

Research funding for individual scientist

➡️ ERC Synergy Grant — European Research Council (ERC) 🇪🇺 Budget: up to €10M (+ €4M with justification) Deadline: November 2026 (expected) Best for: PIs ready to lead a genuinely collaborative, multi-investigator project

➡️ ERC Advanced Grant — European Research Council (ERC) 🇪🇺 Budget: €2.5M Deadline: 27 August 2026 Best for: senior researchers with a strong independent track record looking to pursue high-risk, high-gain science.

➡️ NWO Vidi (full proposal) — NWO (Dutch Research Council) 🇳🇱Budget: €850k Deadline: 9 April 2026 Ideal for: researchers 2–5 years post-PhD, based at or affiliated with a Dutch institution.

➡️ Villum Investigator — Villum Fonden 🇩🇰 Budget: DKK 30M Deadline: 20 August 2026 Ideal for: top-tier senior researchers with a proven record of independent, curiosity-driven research.

➡️ ERC Proof of Concept — European Research Council (ERC) 🇪🇺 Budget: €150k Deadline: 17 September 2026 Ideal for: current or former ERC grant holders looking to explore the commercial or societal potential of their results.

Research & innovation funding for research projects

➡️ EIC Pathfinder Open — EIC - European Innovation Council 🇪🇺 Budget: €4M Deadline: 12 May 2026 Ideal for: multi-partner consortia working on foundational research with a clear path to breakthrough technology.

➡️ EIC Pathfinder Challenges — EIC - European Innovation Council 🇪🇺 Budget: €4M Deadline: 28 October 2026 Ideal for: teams whose research aligns with one of the defined EIC challenge topics — check the call carefully before applying.

➡️ NNF Challenge Programme (Stage 2) — Novo Nordisk Foundation 🇩🇰 Budget: DKK 75M Deadline: 2 June 2026 Ideal for: large, ambitious research programmes that have already passed Stage 1 screening with the Novo Nordisk Foundation.
