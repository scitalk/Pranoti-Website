Ultra-Fast Physics on Under the Microscope 🔬🎧

Sent on 10 October 2024

Attosecond tech, Diversity, DESY & links.

🔬 New Episode out now!🎙️

📸 Fastest Cameras to Capture Electron Dynamics 📸

🎧 Spotify

About the guest

Francesca Calegari is a professor at the University of Hamburg and DESY. She is a leading expert in the field of ultra-fast light pulses and attosecond technology, which is transforming our understanding of electron dynamics and light-matter interactions.

Podcast covers

✨ Attosecond Technology: Discover how this cutting-edge method captures the rapid movements of electrons, revealing the hidden dynamics of matter.

🔬 DESY’s Role: Learn about DESY’s critical contributions to X-ray research and how it supports breakthroughs in materials science.

🚀 Ultra-Fast Spectroscopy: Find out how this technique is unlocking new insights into the properties of materials at ultra-short timescales.

🌍 Diversity in Science: Francesca highlights the importance of fostering diverse perspectives in research and mentoring the next generation of scientists.
