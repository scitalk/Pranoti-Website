The background

I'm a materials scientist by training — PhD from the University of Tübingen, research on graphene-based transparent electrodes at the Natural and Medical Sciences Institute — who spent several years at the intersection of deep-tech science and commercial product strategy at Springer Nature, before making a full pivot into science communication consulting.

In 2018, I founded The Science Talk, a science communication platform built around the conviction that researchers deserve better tools for talking about their work. Over the past seven years, I've grown it from a side project into a business with four product lines, an official EIC partnership, and a podcast that has reached scientists in 20+ countries.

Since late 2025, I've been adding a second specialisation: AI integration and automation. Not as a generalist tool coach, but with a specific focus on the operational infrastructure that research organisations need to work efficiently — proposal workflows, communication pipelines, content systems — built with Claude API and Make.

I work in English and German, hold a Mini-MBA in Marketing from Marketing Week, and have been recognised with the IOP Award for Innovative Public Engagement across UK and Europe.
